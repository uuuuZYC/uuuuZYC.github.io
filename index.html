About

I am an incoming Ph.D. student (Fall 2027) and a member of the PKU-VaLuE Lab (Vision-Language and Embodied AI), advised by Prof. Yiwu Zhong at Peking University. My current research focuses on embodied AI, in particular vision-language-action (VLA) models and world action models (WAMs).

I am completing my B.Eng. in Software Engineering at Southeast University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yang Chen (School of Computer Science and Engineering) and Prof. Guangquan Zhou (School of Biological Science and Medical Engineering). There I worked on medical image analysis and vision-language models, including training-free methods for visually grounded multimodal reasoning.
